# SUPPORT

**Type Natural Korean: Keyboard**

Having trouble enabling the keyboard, found a wrong correction or translation, or want to suggest a feature? We read everything.

- Email: [jjw3435@gmail.com](mailto:jjw3435@gmail.com)

When reporting a wrong or missed correction or translation, please include the sentence you checked — it directly helps us improve the keyboard.

## Enabling the keyboard

1. Open **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard...**
2. Tap **Natural Korean**.
3. Back in the Keyboards list, tap **Natural Korean** again and turn on **Allow Full Access**. Full Access is only needed for Check and Translate — typing works without it.

## Switching to it

While typing in any app, tap the globe key 🌐 on the keyboard to switch to Natural Korean, and tap it again to switch to another keyboard.

## FAQ

**Why does it ask for Full Access?**
Full Access is required only so Check and Translate can send a network request to Google's Gemini API. It is not used to read other apps, log your keystrokes, or track what you type outside the current field. Typing Korean and English works fully offline without it.

**What gets sent, and when?**
Only when you tap Check or Translate: the text of the field you're editing (or your selected text, up to 1,500 characters) is sent over an encrypted connection directly from your device to Google's Gemini API. We operate no server and store nothing. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**How do I remove the keyboard?**
Go to **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Edit**, tap the red minus button next to **Natural Korean**, then tap **Delete**.
