# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated October 22, 2026**

This Privacy Notice for unglazed ("**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("**process**") your information when you use our services ("**Services**"), including when you:

  * Download and use our mobile application and keyboard extension (Type Natural Korean: Keyboard, shown on your device as "Natural Korean"), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice

  * Engage with us in other related ways, including any support conversations

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [jjw3435@gmail.com](mailto:jjw3435@gmail.com).

## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

**What personal information do we process?** Almost none. Natural Korean has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or Apple's advertising (IDFA) or vendor (IDFV) identifiers. The only data we collect is usage statistics, described below.

**What happens when I type?** The text you type never leaves your device. Typing Korean (두벌식) and English works fully offline and does not require Full Access.

**What happens when I tap Check or Translate?** Only then, and only with Full Access turned on, the text of the field you're editing — or, if you've selected text, just that selection, up to 1,500 characters — goes over an encrypted connection straight to Google's Gemini API. **We operate no server, and we do not receive, log, or store that text.** Google processes the request under its own API terms ([Google APIs Terms of Service](https://developers.google.com/terms) and the [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)). The result appears in the keyboard and is only written into your text field if you tap Accept (Check) or Apply (Translate) — nothing is changed automatically. Do not enter passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information into Check or Translate.

**What is Full Access for?** iOS shows its own warning before you can turn Full Access on, and we use it for exactly two things: letting Check and Translate make a network request to Google's Gemini API, and sending the usage statistics described below. We do not use it to read other apps, to read anything beyond the current text field's context that iOS gives to keyboards, to log your keystrokes, or to build a history.

**Do you keep any history?** No. There is no check history, no translation history, and no accounts.

**Do you use cookies or have a website?** No. There is no website version — Natural Korean is an iOS keyboard extension and a small companion app only.

**Do we use analytics, tracking, or ads?** We use usage statistics. We do not track you and we show no ads. Starting with version 1.2, the keyboard and the companion app send usage statistics to Mixpanel, Inc. (United States) so we can understand how the keyboard is used and improve it: event names and counts (for example, keyboard opened or Check tapped), the character count of the text you submit (a number, never the text), the tone or context you chose, the kind of result, app and OS version, device model, and a random install identifier. Mixpanel derives country, region, and city from your IP address and then discards the IP address. We never send what you type, your field contents, keystrokes, corrections, or translations. We use no Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and no vendor identifier (IDFV), we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we never sell your information. See Section 1 for the full list.

**Do we collect any information from third parties?** No.

**What do you store on my device?** Only your goal — the politeness and context you set for Translate — your toolbar padding preference, and a random install identifier, kept in storage on your device that the app and the keyboard share. The install identifier and the tone or context you choose are included in usage statistics (see above); your toolbar padding preference is never sent.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE PROCESS?

**Text you submit for Check or Translate.** When you tap Check or Translate — both require Full Access and a network connection — the text of the field you're editing (or, if you've selected text, just that selection), up to 1,500 characters, is sent over an encrypted connection directly from your device to Google's Gemini API solely to produce the grammar check or translation shown to you. We operate no server: we neither receive, log, nor store that text. The result appears in the keyboard and is only written into your text field if you tap Accept or Apply.

**Full Access.** We use Full Access for exactly two purposes: making the network request to Google's Gemini API when you tap Check or Translate, and sending usage statistics to Mixpanel (see "Usage statistics" below). We do not use it to read other apps, to read text beyond the current field's context that iOS exposes to keyboards, to log your keystrokes, or to build a history. Ordinary typing — Korean (두벌식) and English — works fully offline and never requires Full Access.

**Usage statistics.** Starting with version 1.2, the keyboard extension and the companion app send usage statistics to Mixpanel, Inc. (United States) so we can understand how the keyboard is used and improve it. The statistics are:

  * Event names and counts: keyboard opened, Check or Translate tapped, result shown, suggestion accepted or dismissed, translation applied, error shown, app opened, and setup step reached

  * The character count of the text you submit — a number, never the text itself

  * The tone or context you chose

  * The kind of result: the verdict, the error category, or the detected source language

  * App version, OS version, and device model

  * A random install identifier that the app and the keyboard share, so that one person is counted once

Mixpanel derives country, region, and city from your IP address and then discards the IP address. We never send the text you type, your field contents, keystrokes, corrections, or translations. We use no Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and no vendor identifier (IDFV), we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we show no ads. The keyboard can send usage statistics only when Full Access is on; without it, the keyboard has no network access. There is no in-app switch for usage statistics; deleting the app and keyboard stops collection (see Section 6).

**On-device data.** The app and the keyboard store three things locally, in storage on your device that they share: your chosen goal (the politeness and context you set for Translate), your toolbar padding preference, and the random install identifier. The install identifier and the tone or context you choose are included in usage statistics, as described above; your toolbar padding preference is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We do not process any other personal information. There are no accounts and no history. Apart from the usage statistics described above, we run no analytics and no tracking.

## 2. LEGAL BASES AND PURPOSE

We process the text you submit only to provide the core function of the keyboard — checking Korean grammar or translating English into Korean — at your explicit request each time you tap Check or Translate. We do not use it for any other purpose.

## 3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

We share information with two third parties. **Google LLC** (Gemini API) is the only third party that receives the text you submit, strictly to generate the check or translation result. **Mixpanel, Inc.** (United States) is our processor for usage statistics only; it receives the usage statistics described in Section 1 and never the text you submit. Each of these third parties provides the same or equal protection of your data as stated in this Privacy Notice. We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. We have no advertising partners and no data brokers.

## 4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We keep none of your content: we operate no server that stores it. Your on-device goal and toolbar padding preferences are retained until you change them or delete the app and keyboard, and the random install identifier is retained until you delete the app and keyboard. Usage statistics sent to Mixpanel are retained there for 2 years. Google's transient processing of Gemini API requests is governed by Google's terms linked above.

## 5. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

All network requests made by Check and Translate use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We store nothing on our servers — we operate none.

## 6. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on your region (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability. Because we hold so little, most requests can be satisfied by you alone: deleting the app and keyboard removes the goal, toolbar padding preference, and random install identifier stored on your device, and stops all collection of usage statistics. There is no in-app switch for usage statistics. For anything else, including requests about usage statistics, contact us at [jjw3435@gmail.com](mailto:jjw3435@gmail.com) and we will respond within 30 days.

## 7. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 18 years of age. Neither the app nor the keyboard asks for an account, a name, or any contact details.
